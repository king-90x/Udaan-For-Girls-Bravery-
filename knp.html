Boundaries Without Guilt

Boundary doosron ko rokne ke liye nahi, khud ko surakshit rakhne ke liye hoti hai.

Time

Padhai aur aaram ke liye apna waqt tay karna.

Privacy

Phone, diary aur messages aapke hain.

Personal space

Kamra, saaman aur sharir par aapka haq.

Emotional

Har baat ka jawab dena zaroori nahi.

The Power of Saying No

"Na" ek poora vakya hai. Use samjhane ke liye lamba bhashan zaroori nahi.

"Main comfortable nahi hoon."
"Mujhe sochne ka time chahiye."
"Mujhe ye pasand nahi aa raha, please ruk jaiye."
"Abhi nahi, shayad baad mein baat karte hain."

Healthy Relationships

Dosti ho ya parivaar, achha rishta aapko chhota nahi, bada mehsoos karata hai.

  • ✓ Respect: aapki baat ko gambhirta se liya jata hai
  • ✓ Trust: dar ke bina sach bol sakti hain
  • ✓ Personal space: alag rehne par bhi rishta safe hai
  • ✓ Disagreement: bina chillaye baat hoti hai

When Someone Crosses a Boundary

1

Identify

Agar kuch galat lag raha hai, to us feeling par bharosa kijiye.

2

Respond clearly

Seedhe aur shaant shabdon mein kahiye: "Ye mujhe pasand nahi, please band kijiye."

3

Take trusted support

Parent, teacher ya counsellor ko batayiye. Aap akeli nahi hain, aur ye aapki galti nahi hai.
