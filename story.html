The Courage to Learn

Meera ne pehli baar coding class mein kadam rakha, aur use laga ki baaki sab usse aage hain. Pehle hafte wo chup rahi. Phir usne ek sawal likha aur haath utha diya. Teacher ne kaha, "Achha sawal hai." Dheere-dheere sawal poochna uski aadat ban gayi.

Lesson: Beginner hona sharm ki baat nahi hai. Sawal wahi poochta hai jo seekhna chahta hai.

Illustrative story

The Courage to Speak

Zoya ke group project mein uski mehnat ka credit kisi aur ko mil gaya. Usne pehle ek dost se baat ki, phir apne notes taiyaar kiye, aur shaanti se teacher ko samjhaya. Uski baat suni gayi aur credit sahi hua.

Lesson: Bolna akele nahi hona chahiye. Tayyari aur trusted support se awaaz majboot hoti hai.

Illustrative story

The Courage to Begin Again

Anaya ka exam kharab gaya aur wo kai din tak kisi se nahi boli. Phir usne ek page par likha ki kya galat hua. Agle hafte usne ek chhota study plan banaya. Result turant nahi badla, par kadam aage badhne laga.

Lesson: Setback ke baad pehla kadam chhota ho sakta hai, bas shuru hona chahiye.
